    A little help on your route
    Move: WASD, arrow keys, or the touch arrows. Click or tap an open patch of ground to walk there.
    Interact: Stand near a mailbox or service and press E, Space, or the on-screen action button.
    Find your way: Match the packet’s destination to a device’s address. The hint button points you toward your next stop. The house button returns you to the post office.
    Your completed deliveries save automatically on this browser when storage is available. Sound starts off.
    This is a teaching simulation: your postman represents delivery; envelopes are packets. Real packets are forwarded electronically, and physical distance doesn’t define subnet membership. Addresses and hostnames here are illustrative.
    
  
    Right device. Which application?
    The destination IP identifies the device. The destination port identifies a service on that device. Your packet needs HTTPS, port 443.
